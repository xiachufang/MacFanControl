# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

Mac Fan Control is a local macOS utility for viewing fan speed and applying fan mode presets.

## Data Collection

Mac Fan Control does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app reads hardware fan information locally on your Mac. Fan readings, mode selections, and helper communication remain on the device. The app does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, third-party data collection, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

## Privileged Helper

Mac Fan Control may ask permission to install a privileged helper so it can apply local fan mode changes. The helper communicates with the app on the same Mac and does not send data to any external service.

## Network Use

Mac Fan Control does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.
